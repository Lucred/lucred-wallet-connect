What the LCRD score reads

Every point in an LCRD score traces back to real, verifiable, on-chain activity. We only index protocols that are live on Stellar mainnet, active, and emitting on-chain events — no off-chain data, no self-reported information. This page is the full, honest list of what feeds the score today, and what we'll fold in next.
